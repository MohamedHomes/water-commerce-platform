# UX Principles

## 1. Fast ordering

The customer journey minimizes friction for recurring orders and standard product purchase flows.

## 2. Minimal taps

Critical actions such as buy again, repeat last order, and checkout are designed for high-speed completion.

## 3. Clear pricing

All totals, delivery fees, taxes, and discounts must be visible before payment confirmation.

## 4. Trust and visibility

Users can easily see order, delivery, and payment status without confusion.

## 5. Accessibility-first design

The interface must support right-to-left and left-to-right layouts while preserving readability, contrast, and touch usability.

## 6. Product clarity

Variants, packaging, volume, and quantity are explicit so users can make confident decisions.

## 7. Repeat purchase optimization

The app emphasizes buy again, reorder, and repeat last order workflows for a recurring-consumption product.
